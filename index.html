Our Story

About iSmart
Digital Connect

iSmart Digital Connect was founded by Basorun Idris, a Lagos-born tech entrepreneur, in October 2023. He began building at 17 — iterating through multiple solutions before achieving traction with Rewarder, a campus ticketing app.

Our mission is to revolutionize the tech space by building apps that solve real problems, empower upcoming creators, and make digital content accessible worldwide.

Pose is our flagship global product — designed to give Nigerian and African creators the fair earnings they deserve, while connecting them with audiences everywhere.
